# AI-Based Online Food Donation Platform

An AI-powered web platform designed to connect food donors with NGOs and volunteers to reduce food wastage and help distribute surplus food to people in need.

## 📌 Project Overview

The **AI-Based Online Food Donation Platform** provides a centralized system where donors can list surplus food, NGOs can find and request available food, and volunteers can assist with food pickup and delivery.

The platform aims to make food donation more organized, efficient, and accessible while supporting AI-based features for smarter food management.

## 🎯 Objectives

- Reduce food wastage by connecting surplus food with organizations in need.
- Provide an easy-to-use platform for food donors.
- Help NGOs discover and request available food donations.
- Support volunteers in managing pickup and delivery activities.
- Provide location-based functionality for connecting nearby users.
- Integrate AI-based features for intelligent food management.

## 👥 User Roles

### 1. Donor

Donors can:

- Register and log in.
- Add food donation details.
- Provide information such as food name, quantity, location, and availability.
- View their submitted donations.
- Track the status of their donations.

### 2. NGO

NGOs can:

- Register and log in.
- Browse available food donations.
- View food donation details.
- Request available food.
- Track their food requests.

### 3. Volunteer

Volunteers can:

- Access a volunteer dashboard.
- View assigned pickup and delivery tasks.
- Assist with transportation of donated food.
- Manage pickup and delivery activities.

## ✨ Key Features

- User registration and login
